# Flamingo Recap Studio Vision

Flamingo is one creator's private research, production and audience-learning studio. Its purpose is excellent, accurate, entertaining manhwa recaps that attract the right viewers, sustain attention, earn return visits and subscriptions, and require fewer manual corrections. It is not a public SaaS, billing system or high-volume upload factory.

Flamingo should operate as a private television production company: a manhwa is a series, each chapter an episode, and a multi-chapter recap a compilation or feature edition. The Series Bible is the authoritative continuity resource; structured episode evidence connects writing, narration, editing, QA and promotion. External research informs programming before ingestion, and measured audience response informs future research and creative decisions.

The creator is creative director and final publishing authority. Automation should act like a coordinated research team, story analyst, OCR specialist, writer/editor, voice director, visual editor, quality director, packaging strategist and audience analyst. It must make uncertainty and evidence visible, respect spending limits, and preserve meaningful human decisions.

## Integrated workflow

1. **Research opportunities before ingestion:** investigate series and broader genres/topics beyond the current uploads, watchlist and scraper sources. Use official catalogs/rankings/new releases, permitted community discussions, public YouTube competitors/search/comparables, accessible search-interest data, official news/returns/hiatuses/adaptations and verified, authorized vidIQ capabilities. Record cited observations and dates before conclusions. Distinguish recent measured changes from lifetime averages, provider estimates and inference.
2. **Understand the source:** preserve artwork, reading order, dialogue, captions, actions, reactions, reveals, characters and uncertain speakers. Keep visible evidence separate from inference and canonical series context. Trace source lines and events into narration.
3. **Write compelling stories:** accurate openings, cause and effect, characterization, meaningful dialogue, natural transitions, suspense, emotional variation and appropriate personality. Avoid invented events, repeated explanations, inconsistent names and mechanical descriptions of artwork.
4. **Direct voice and sound:** clear, natural speech, stable voices, appropriate pronunciation, deliberate scene pauses and clean transitions. Measure actual audio; do not mask missing voice with music or apply global acceleration as a substitute for editing.
5. **Edit visually:** choose images and crops for the spoken event; differentiate action, dialogue, reactions and reveals; keep tall artwork readable; vary motion purposefully without excessive effects. Preserve artwork and the working rendering pipeline.
6. **Assure quality:** combine source, narrative, structural and delivered-media checks. Findings need severity, confidence, timestamps, affected source/beat/segment IDs and focused repair suggestions. A report must identify what it actually inspected. Incomplete checks are not passes.
7. **Package and approve:** generate distinct, accurate title/thumbnail promises supported by chapter evidence; maintain series identity, aliases, playlists and metadata. Treat predicted scores as hypotheses and compare with observed impressions, CTR, retention and subscriber conversion.
8. **Distribute approved assets:** long-form first, then original Shorts and community participation, followed by measured Instagram/Facebook Reels experiments and later X experiments. Vertical stories require their own hooks, structure, crops, captions and QA.
9. **Listen and learn:** summarize comments, requests and complaints; draft personalized replies for approval; connect editorial corrections and observed audience performance to future research, scripts, editing and packaging. Distinguish correlation from causation.

## Shared Series and Episode Intelligence

Extend the existing Series Bible, series packs, watchlist/provider mapping and project provenance; do not create a disconnected second canonical database. Stable identity must outlive a title/slug change. Verify English/original Korean titles, alternative spellings, romanizations and abbreviations; preserve ambiguous matches for review instead of counting one work repeatedly or merging different works/adaptations.

Series Intelligence connects canonical identity, genre/themes/premise, characters/relationships/terms, worldbuilding/continuity, official publication/release metadata, evidence/confidence/disputes, known episodes, external-interest/competitor observations, audience hypotheses, approved recurring presentation/thumbnail style and production/publication history. Separate market evidence and theories from approved story canon. A Reddit theory or competitor claim cannot silently become a factual character or plot field. Preserve owner corrections and their origin.

Episode Intelligence connects original ordered pages, image references/hashes, panel IDs, ordered typed OCR/captions/dialogue/SFX, speaker identity/evidence basis, characters/locations, events/reactions/intentions/consequences, scenes/transitions, reveals/stakes/hooks, source-line/panel provenance, uncertainty, missing evidence and editorial corrections. Distinguish observations from inferred intent or emotion and keep a versioned relationship to the Bible and source edition. Compilations retain their child episode identities and offsets.

The same evidence/revisions must feed script writing and revision, dialogue coverage/factual checks, pronunciation/TTS, narration-to-panel matching, shot selection/timing/cropping, final QA, SEO/descriptions, thumbnail concepts, Shorts/Reels/social preparation and audience follow-up/continuity. Writers know what art/dialogue supports; editors know what narration means; narration timing carries emotional and story intent. Models consume the shared record and return referenced draft claims rather than invent separate versions of the story. References alone are not semantic verification.

A metadata/source/script correction should invalidate only outputs that depend on it. Preserve unchanged voice/media and operator edits; show affected results and cost before a paid rerun. Market observations must not invalidate story canon or force new narration. Exact-export and packaging approvals follow their own artifact revisions. Current whole-artifact cache checks are a foundation, not completed selective invalidation.

## Research evidence and programming decisions

Persist evidence, not only an AI conclusion. Every opportunity names the series/topic, verifies aliases, explains its audience and why it matters, cites source URLs with observation dates, separates measurements/estimates/inferences, states any verifiable recent-interest change, identifies comparable videos and their publication age/format, discusses sampled competition and uncertainty, characterizes demand as broad/niche/emerging/uncertain with reasons, checks suitable chapters/lawful material, estimates effort with a basis, lists risks/missing evidence and explains why to choose it now over another named candidate.

Publisher popularity, Reddit activity, YouTube views and Google search interest are distinct signals. Never invent search volumes, velocity, forecasts or popularity scores. Prefer independent corroboration; expose disagreements and repeated-source dependence. Catalog availability alone establishes neither market demand nor reuse permission. Intended-channel identity, available connector tools, costs and access must be verified before research calls; no automatic account reconnection or paid collection.

## Packaging truth and performance

Keep **market research** (audience/comparable evidence), **episode research** (what occurs and why it matters), and **packaging strategy** (a truthful, distinctive promise) separate and linked. Prepare multiple distinct title/thumbnail concepts grounded in real episode events or verified series themes. Review clarity, specificity, appeal, mobile readability, visual distinctiveness, relevant terms and whether the opening delivers the hook.

Prepare accurate descriptions, canonical/alternative-title references, chapter/compilation information, relevant tags, useful playlist association and justified hashtags. Reuse existing approved branding and locked-series-hook controls; new concepts remain drafts until chosen, and cannot silently retitle earlier work. Heuristic/model rubric scores are not verified performance forecasts. When authorized, use observed CTR, watch time, retention, returning viewers and experiment outcomes with sample/window context.

## Formats and operator experience

Support chapter recaps and multi-chapter compilations. Eventually, one approved recap can produce two distinct vertical mini-stories, a character discussion, a poll, reply suggestions and platform-specific metadata. Each asset needs an objective: discovery, long-form conversion, conversation or returning-viewer engagement.

Use the existing Home, Library, Chapter, Queue, Activity and Settings surfaces. A unified brief should summarize research evidence, production progress, actionable quality defects, packaging choices, assets awaiting approval and observed performance. Do not create another disconnected dashboard or queue.

The connected workspaces are Research Desk, Series Room, Episode Production, Quality Director, Packaging Studio, Publishing Queue and Audience Desk. Map them onto the current Home/Library series views, Chapter Board/Issues/Video, packaging controls, Queue and Activity before adding navigation. Evidence and series/episode IDs should connect every decision. One approved episode can support coordinated promotional assets, with independent preview/QA/approval for each.

Mangayomi, NewPipe and Stremio are resources for verified capability/UI assessment, not assumed demand engines. Extend only justified catalog/metadata patterns; do not add unsafe extensions, unauthorized access or unnecessary streaming/download systems. The referenced resource video was not supplied with this text addendum, so its exact contents remain unassessed.

Keep one main Flamingo channel as the baseline, with genre/series playlists. Additional channels need evidence about audience overlap and production capacity. Resolve canonical channel IDs and connected profiles before altering any destination; account labels alone are insufficient.

## Engineering and authority boundaries

Preserve working ingestion, source fallbacks, Series Bible, structured OCR, anchored scripts, matching, timing repair, renderers, packaging, usage limits, undo and publishing controls. Prefer focused improvements and shared interfaces to replacement subsystems.

Development may proceed autonomously on feature branches with offline tests. Significant work is appended to `manhwa-recap-v1/memory.md` and committed/pushed at checkpoints. Never stage scraped art, generated proprietary media, credentials or private account evidence.

Without separate owner authorization: no main push/merge, deployment, production-data mutation, paid AI request, real chapter regeneration, account change or public publishing/scheduling. No engagement bots or unsolicited automatic replies. Source permissions and publishing authority must remain explicit; boilerplate disclaimers do not establish asset rights.

New technical publishing enforcement remains off by default until calibrated against real Flamingo media and explicitly approved. Mark offline implementations Ready for Review, not production-complete. Preserve original media and corrections; make repairs targeted, reversible and cost-bounded.

Implementation tasks, evidence and unresolved decisions belong in [the development roadmap](FLAMINGO_DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md). Detailed operational behavior belongs in `manhwa-recap-v1/PIPELINE_HANDBOOK.md`; append-only session history belongs in `memory.md`.

The [connected-intelligence design and source assessment](FLAMINGO_INTELLIGENCE_DESIGN.md) records current reuse/repair/build findings, proposed data contracts, source access limits and UI mapping. These plans do not claim that the new intelligence engine is implemented. Preserve the eight milestones and finish ongoing approved offline work before dependent implementations.
